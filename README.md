## 📋 Project Overview

This Power BI dashboard analyzes a company’s online retail transactions—including invoices, products, sales dates, prices, and quantities. 

The primary goal is to uncover hidden patterns and key performance indicators (KPIs) that empower executive leadership. It provides tailored, actionable strategies designed specifically for the **CEO** (focusing on revenue, growth, and refund impact) and the **CMO** (focusing on customer behavior, product performance, and seasonality).

---

## 🎯 Executive Summaries

### 💼 Key Insights for the CEO (Revenue & Growth)

#### Total Sales Revenue & Average Order Value (AOV) – 2011
- **Topline Metrics:** $9.75M Total Revenue | ~26K Orders | **$376 AOV**
- **Strategic Opportunity:** Increasing AOV by just 10% can unlock **~$1M in additional revenue** without acquiring a single new customer.
- **Actionable Advice:** Implement upselling, cross-selling, dynamic product bundles, and tiered loyalty rewards.

#### Revenue Growth Over Time
- **Quarterly Trends:** Consistent growth from Q1 to Q2 (12% increase, $1.7M → $1.9M).
- **Forecasting:** Expected year-end revenue is on track to land **+$400K above forecast** if current trends hold.
- **YoY Explosion:** Q4 demonstrated dramatic growth from 2010 to 2011 (**328% YoY**).

#### Highest Revenue-Generating Products
- **Revenue Distribution:** The top 5 products contribute only 7.3% of total revenue, indicating a **highly diversified** and resilient revenue base.
- **Top Performer:** Product DOT leads the catalog with $206,245 in revenue.
- **Actionable Advice:** Balance promotion between reliable top products and high-value long-tail items.

#### Refund & Return Impact ⚠️
- **The True Cost:** Refunds cost the company **$897K (~9.2% of gross revenue)**.
- **Realized Revenue:** Net revenue sits at $8.85M, not the gross $9.75M.
- **Actionable Advice:** Immediate need to investigate high-return products, regional quality control issues, and delivery-related complaints to recover this margin.

---

### 📈 Key Insights for the CMO (Customer Behavior & Marketing)

#### Most Popular Products (Volume)
- **Top Mover:** Product 23843 is the highest-volume item (80,995 units sold generating $168,469.60).
- **Strategic Use:** Ideal anchor product for traffic-driver campaigns, loss-leader bundling, and behavior-based targeting.

#### Seasonal Demand Patterns
- **The Q4 Surge:** Sales grow steadily throughout the year, culminating in a massive peak of **$3.7M in Q4**.
- **Actionable Advice:** Q4 is make-or-break. Requires aggressive scaling of ad spend, promotional campaigns, inventory boosting, and emphasizing giftable bundles.

#### Repeat Purchase Behavior & Loyalty
- **Retention:** Strong baseline repeat rate of **31–32%**.
- **Vulnerabilities:** Slight dips observed in February and April.
- **Actionable Advice:** Deploy personalized re-engagement email flows, launch point-based loyalty programs, and incentivize post-purchase offers.

#### Average Basket Size
- **Customer Profile:** The average order contains **~199 items**, highlighting a strong B2B or bulk-buying customer base.
- **Actionable Advice:** Create VIP retention programs for top-volume buyers while testing strategies to increase purchase frequency among smaller-order segments.

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations

### For Leadership (CEO)
1. **Drive AOV:** Implement upsell bundles to capture the $1M+ hidden revenue opportunity.
2. **Mitigate Loss:** Audit the return pipeline to systematically reduce refunds and recover up to $900K.
3. **Category Expansion:** Double down on high-potential, high-margin product categories.
4. **Target Optimization:** Closely monitor seasonality to adjust quarterly revenue targets accurately.

### For Marketing (CMO)
1. **Q4 Amplification:** Front-load marketing budgets to maximize the highest-demand period.
2. **Traffic generation:** Utilize top-volume products as the tip-of-the-spear in acquisition campaigns.
3. **LTV Expansion:** Elevate repeat-purchase strategies to lift overall Customer Lifetime Value.
4. **Segmentation:** Build highly targeted offers explicitly for high-spending, bulk-buying cohorts.
